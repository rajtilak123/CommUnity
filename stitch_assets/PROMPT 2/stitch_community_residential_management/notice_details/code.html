Planned Water Shut-off for Block B

Dear Residents,

Please be informed that there will be a scheduled water supply interruption on Thursday, November 2nd, 2023, between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM.

This interruption is necessary to perform essential upgrade maintenance on the main pumping station located in the basement of Block B. During this time, both hot and cold water services will be entirely unavailable across all apartments in Block B.

info We highly recommend storing sufficient water for drinking, cooking, and basic sanitary needs prior to the shut-off time to minimize disruption.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and sincerely appreciate your cooperation as we work to maintain and improve the building's infrastructure. If you have any immediate concerns or require special assistance during this window, please contact the facilities helpdesk immediately.

Sincerely,
The Property Management Team
